Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026. This policy is for the Mimir Kids Arcade and Learning app (iPhone and iPad), published by Kevin Clark.

We do not collect your data

Mimir does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you or your children across apps or websites. The app’s privacy manifest declares that it does not track and does not collect data types.

No ads and no tracking

There are no ads in Mimir. We do not sell, share, or broker information, because we do not collect it.

What stays on the device

Player profiles, game progress, settings, and (if you use them) parent voice recordings for Hej Dansk are stored on the device. They are not uploaded to us. If you delete the app, that on-device information is removed with it, unless Apple backups you have enabled include the app’s data.

Purchases

Optional learning, language, and game packs are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never see or store your Apple ID password, payment card, or billing details. Manage or restore purchases with your Apple ID and the Restore Purchases button in Parents → Packs. A parent PIN protects the Parents area, including purchases.

Microphone

The microphone is used only from the PIN-protected parent tools in Hej Dansk, so a parent can record their own voice for words and phrases. Recordings stay on the device and are never uploaded to us.

Children

Mimir is designed for families with young children. Kids cannot reach purchase screens. We do not create accounts for children and do not collect children’s personal information.

Internet use

Gameplay works offline. The app talks to Apple only as needed for App Store purchases and restore. It does not include an open web browser or unrestricted web access.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about information on your device, can be sent to kevin@texasviking.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
