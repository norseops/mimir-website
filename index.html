Arcade games plus lessons, with no ads and no tracking.

Mimir is a polished arcade and learning app for families with kids around five to eight. Six games come with the app. Optional learning, language, and game packs are sold as one-time in-app purchases, behind a parent PIN.

Included games

Hay Hero, Puzzles, Coloring Book, Hill Rider, Hungry Hole, and Sky Sling.

Optional packs

Game Pack 2, learning packs for Pre-K through 2nd Grade, and language packs for Danish (Hej Dansk) and Spanish (¡Hola!).

For parents

There is no account to create. Play works offline. Purchases stay in the Parents area, protected by a PIN. Mimir does not collect personal data, show ads, or track anyone.
